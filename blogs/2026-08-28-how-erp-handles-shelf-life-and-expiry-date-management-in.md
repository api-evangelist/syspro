In food manufacturing, expiry date and shelf life management are not optional. They are a regulatory requirement that directly impacts consumer safety and brand reputation. Compliance frameworks such as HACCP, ISO 22000 and regional food safety laws demand precise control over shelf life, batch tracking and traceability.
