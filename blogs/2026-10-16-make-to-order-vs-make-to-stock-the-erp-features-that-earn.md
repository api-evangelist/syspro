Every enterprise resource planning (ERP) vendor says it supports both make-to-order and make-to-stock production. Run a make-to-order (MTO) shop inside a system designed for make-to-stock (MTS), though, and the claim breaks down in year one. Custom jobs get forced through workflows built for repeatable runs.
