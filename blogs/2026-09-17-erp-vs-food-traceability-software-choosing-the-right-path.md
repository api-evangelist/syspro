Food and beverage manufacturers are under growing pressure to balance compliance, efficiency and consumer trust. Global supply chains have become more complex, while regulators demand ever‑stricter transparency into ingredient origins, batch records and recall readiness. At the same time, industry trends such as sustainability, clean‑label products are reshaping how manufacturers view food traceability.
